# Interface Directions

Use this file only when you need help choosing or defending a direction.

## Precision & Density

- Best for dashboards, admin panels, developer tools, and data-dense product surfaces.
- Favor tight spacing, crisp borders, restrained color, and minimal decorative shadow.
- Use when clarity and throughput matter more than softness.

## Warmth & Approachability

- Best for collaborative tools, onboarding, and consumer-facing application flows.
- Favor softer radius, gentler depth, and more generous spacing.
- Use when the interface should feel inviting without becoming playful or marketing-led.

## Sophistication & Trust

- Best for finance, enterprise, settings, and high-confidence workflows.
- Favor cool neutrals, layered surfaces, strong information hierarchy, and disciplined accent usage.
- Use when credibility and calm control matter most.

## Boldness & Clarity

- Best for modern analytics, operational views, and products that need strong emphasis.
- Favor stronger contrast, cleaner section breaks, and more assertive spacing changes.
- Use when the UI needs visual energy without losing structure.

## Utility & Function

- Best for GitHub-style tools and functional CRUD-heavy interfaces.
- Favor muted color, direct affordances, and compact patterns.
- Use when ornamental polish would distract from the task.

## Data & Analysis

- Best for BI surfaces, chart-heavy products, and interfaces dominated by metrics.
- Favor chart legibility, table rhythm, numeric hierarchy, and disciplined density.
- Use when the interface exists primarily to scan, compare, and decide.

## Selection Heuristics

- If users spend most of their time managing records or scanning dense content, start with `Precision & Density` or `Utility & Function`.
- If the product handles sensitive decisions or account settings, bias toward `Sophistication & Trust`.
- If charts and KPI scanning dominate the experience, bias toward `Data & Analysis`.
- If the product needs more emotional softness for teamwork or onboarding, bias toward `Warmth & Approachability`.
- If the product needs stronger visual emphasis or modern contrast, bias toward `Boldness & Clarity`.
